* 3/4 oz Lime Juice
* 3/4 oz Orgeat
* 1/4 oz Simple Syrup
* 5 Dash Chocolate Bitters

I would have thought this cocktail would be sweet but the lime juice goes a long way. I wish I had chocolate bitters I think it would have balanced the cocktail.

I love a good grenadine cocktail and I count this one among them. In the book the description calls for the “whiskey has finally settled deep into the embrace of the pomegranate” and I can’t think of a better description of this cocktail. I might fiddle with the lemon juice to grenadine ration, I think I want even more of that pomegranate tartness. 

{{< figure src="/images/2026-07-13-daisy-17.jpg" >}}


* 2 oz Rye
* 3/4 oz Lemon Juice
* 1/2 oz Grenadine
* 1/4 oz Simple Syrup
* 3 Dash of Bitters

Via [The Bartender’s Manifesto](https://www.amazon.com/Bartenders-Manifesto-Think-Create-Cocktails/dp/0593137981)

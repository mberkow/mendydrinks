Back in the saddle after some urging. Not quite out of practice in mixing but out of practice in posting. I couldn’t believe I had not made this cocktail yet. Well balanced and silky, gorgeous color. 

{{< figure src="/images/2026-06-10-The-Diplomat.jpg" >}}

* 1 1/2 oz Gin
* 3/4 oz Campari
* 3/4 oz Lemon Juice
* 1/2 oz Simple Syrup
* 1 Egg White

Dry shake then shake over ice

Via [Be Your Own Bartender](https://www.amazon.com/Be-Your-Own-Bartender-Surefire/dp/1682682692)

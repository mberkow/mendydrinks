* 1 1/2 oz Rhum
* 1/2 oz Rum
* 1 oz Lime Juice
* 3/4 oz Pineapple Juice
* 3/4 oz Ginger Syrup
* 1/2 oz Orgeat


Another in the nautical theme. Went looking for a recipe with  Ginger Syrup and stumbled across this one that I happened to have all the juices and syrups on hand. It called for Rhum and Rum but I used just Rum. The ginger and pineapple comes through and there is enough sweetness that the spice of the ginger doesn’t overwhelm the drink.

Via [Fredric Yarm](https://cocktailvirgin.blogspot.com/2026/02/set-sail.html)
